# Name and logo

The code of Little Later is MIT-licensed ([LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md)): you may use, change and share it, including in your own extension. The MIT License covers copyright in the code. It grants no rights to the project's name or logo.

## What identifies the official extension

- The name **Little Later**.
- The logos and icons in [`assets/logos-and-animations/`](./assets/logos-and-animations/), `public/icons/` and `public/images/`, and the intro animation in `public/videos/`.

The official extension is published only by the maintainer, [@AndysTMC](https://github.com/AndysTMC): on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/AndysTMC/Little-Later/releases) of this repository and, once listed, on the Chrome Web Store under the maintainer's developer account. Any other listing is not ours.

## You may

- Say that your project is based on, forked from or compatible with Little Later.
- Use the name to refer to this project, for example in an article, a review or a link.
- Keep the name and logo in a fork that you use privately or that only exists to send changes back here.

## Please don't

- Publish a build (on the Chrome Web Store, another extension store or anywhere else) under the name Little Later, a confusingly similar name, or with its logo or icons.
- Present a modified build as the official extension, or suggest that the project endorses yours.

If you publish your own version, change the name, description and icons in `manifest.json` and use your own logo.

## Questions and reports

To ask about a use not covered here, or to report a listing that impersonates Little Later, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
